# Introduction

Palak Paneer is a classic North Indian dish where fresh spinach is cooked with spices and combined with soft paneer cubes. It's vibrant, nutritious, and packed with flavor—perfect for weeknight dinners.

# Ingredients

## For the Spinach Puree
- 1 lb fresh spinach leaves, washed
- 2 green chilies
- 1 inch ginger, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, chopped
- 1/4 cup water

## For the Curry
- 8 oz paneer, cut into cubes
- 2 tbsp oil or ghee
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 2 tomatoes, pureed
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1/2 tsp red chili powder
- 1/2 cup heavy cream or cashew cream
- Salt to taste
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg powder (optional)

# Instructions

1. **Blanch spinach**: Bring a pot of water to boil. Add spinach and blanch for 2 minutes. Immediately transfer to ice water to preserve the green color. Drain well.

2. **Make spinach puree**: In a blender, combine blanched spinach, green chilies, ginger, garlic, and water. Blend until smooth. Set aside.

3. **Prepare paneer**: Lightly fry paneer cubes in oil until golden on all sides. Remove and set aside. (Optional: soak in warm water to keep soft)

4. **Start the curry**: Heat oil in a pan. Add cumin seeds and let them splutter.

5. **Cook onions**: Add chopped onions and sauté until golden brown, about 6-8 minutes.

6. **Add tomatoes**: Pour in tomato puree and cook until oil separates, about 5 minutes.

7. **Add spices**: Stir in garam masala, turmeric, coriander powder, and chili powder. Cook for 1 minute.

8. **Add spinach**: Pour the spinach puree into the pan. Mix well and simmer for 5 minutes.

9. **Add cream**: Stir in heavy cream and mix well. Add salt to taste and nutmeg if using.

10. **Finish**: Add paneer cubes gently. Simmer for 3-4 minutes. Serve hot with roti or rice.

# Tips

- **Keep it green**: Blanching spinach in ice water helps retain the vibrant green color.
- **Paneer alternatives**: Use tofu for a vegan version, or boiled potatoes for a budget-friendly option.
- **Make it richer**: Add a tablespoon of butter at the end for extra richness.
- **Meal prep**: This dish keeps well for 3-4 days in the refrigerator.
- **Serving suggestion**: Garnish with a swirl of cream and serve with garlic naan.
