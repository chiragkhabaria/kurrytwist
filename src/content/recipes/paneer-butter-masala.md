## Ingredients

### For the Paneer:
- 400g paneer, cut into cubes
- 2 tablespoons oil for frying
- 1/4 teaspoon turmeric powder

### For the Gravy:
- 4 large tomatoes, roughly chopped
- 2 medium onions, roughly chopped
- 1-inch ginger piece
- 4-5 garlic cloves
- 2-3 green chilies (adjust to taste)
- 1/2 cup cashew nuts (soaked in warm water)

### For the Curry:
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 1 bay leaf
- 2-3 green cardamom pods
- 1-inch cinnamon stick
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1 teaspoon red chili powder
- 1 teaspoon coriander powder
- 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
- 1 teaspoon kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1 tablespoon sugar (optional, to balance acidity)
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander leaves for garnish

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the Paneer**: Lightly fry the paneer cubes with turmeric in hot oil until golden on all sides. Remove and soak in warm water to keep them soft. Set aside.

2. **Make the Base Paste**: In a blender, combine chopped tomatoes, onions, ginger, garlic, green chilies, and soaked cashews. Blend into a smooth paste. Set aside.

3. **Temper the Spices**: Heat butter in a large pan or kadhai over medium heat. Add bay leaf, cardamom, cinnamon, and cumin seeds. Let them crackle and release their aroma for about 30 seconds.

4. **Cook the Gravy**: Pour the blended tomato-onion paste into the pan. Cook on medium-high heat for 10-12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the oil starts to separate from the gravy and the raw smell disappears.

5. **Add Spices**: Add red chili powder, coriander powder, salt, and sugar. Mix well and cook for another 2-3 minutes to let the spices bloom.

6. **Create the Creamy Texture**: Lower the heat and add the heavy cream, stirring continuously. Crush the kasuri methi between your palms and add it to the curry. Mix well.

7. **Add the Paneer**: Drain the paneer cubes and gently add them to the gravy. Simmer for 3-4 minutes on low heat, allowing the paneer to absorb the flavors.

8. **Finish with Garam Masala**: Sprinkle garam masala over the curry and give it a gentle stir. Turn off the heat.

9. **Garnish and Serve**: Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and a drizzle of cream if desired. Serve hot with naan, roti, or basmati rice.

## Tips

- **For Extra Richness**: Add an extra tablespoon of butter at the end for a restaurant-style finish.
- **Spice Level**: Adjust green chilies and red chili powder according to your preference. This recipe is mildly spiced.
- **Make it Vegan**: Substitute paneer with tofu, use coconut cream instead of dairy cream, and replace butter with vegan butter or oil.
- **Storage**: This curry tastes even better the next day! Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
- **Cashew Alternative**: If you have a nut allergy, you can use melon seeds (magaz) or skip the cashews entirely, though it will be less creamy.
- **Kasuri Methi is Key**: Don't skip the dried fenugreek leaves - they add an authentic, slightly bitter note that elevates the dish.
